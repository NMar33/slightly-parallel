## L2 — Elaboration

Current MySQL → Postgres cutover uses pgloader + dual-write shim. Debezium-based CDC would eliminate the dual-write window entirely. Too late for this migration, but if we document the pattern now, the next two internal DBs migrate on rails.

## L3 — Implications / Next steps

- Post-cutover retro capture what the shim cost us
- Debezium spike in staging in Q4
- Publish `migrations.md` playbook in engineering wiki

## L4 — Raw context

Retros consistently identify the dual-write window as scariest. CDC removes that failure mode. Investment pays back on migration #2.
